Behind the build

Gameplay systems

What I built

  • MovementControlNew reads movement and jump input, updates Rigidbody2D velocity, and switches the Animator between idle and walk.
  • Jumping is gated by contact with the Ground layer; coin and chest triggers update score and feedback.
  • Lift and trap interactions trigger their own animation, collider, audio, and game-over responses.

Challenge

Coordinate platforming controls with pickups, lift behavior, and hazards through separate gameplay components.
